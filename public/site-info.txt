Duas to recite
لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ
Talbiyah: Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am, You have no partner, here I am. All praise and blessings are Yours, and all sovereignty. You have no partner.
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ
Tawaf: “Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire” (Quran 2:201). It is a Sunnah to recite this between the Yemeni Corner and the Black Stone.
إِنَّ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةَ مِن شَعَائِرِ اللَّهِ
Sa'i: “Indeed, Safa and Marwa are among the symbols of Allah” (Quran 2:158). It is recited when you approach Safa for the first time.

## FAQ
Q: Can I book now and pay later?
A: Yes. Reserve your seat first, then complete documents and payment to confirm.
Q: Are flights direct or indirect?
A: Each package shows the flight type, so you can choose.
Q: How far are the hotels from Haram?
A: Every package lists hotel category, distance and walking or shuttle.
Q: Is ziyarat included?
A: Yes — Makkah and Madinah ziyarat are listed in each package.
Q: Can I choose my own hotel and flight?
A: Yes — use Build Your Own Package and tell us your preferences.
Q: Can I travel with my family and children?
A: Yes. Tell us the number of adults and children and we will plan accordingly.
Q: How do I confirm my booking?
A: Send your documents and advance payment as per your package, and we confirm your seat.
Q: What is the difference between Umrah and Hajj?
A: Umrah can be performed at any time of the year and its rites take a few hours. Hajj is obligatory once in a lifetime for those who are able, and is performed on fixed days in the month of Dhul Hijjah.
Q: What are the rites of Umrah?
A: Ihram, Tawaf, Sa'i, and then Halq or Taqsir. See the step-by-step guide on this page.
Q: Do you help with the visa?
A: Yes. Visa processing and follow-up are part of our service. Please keep the documents listed above ready.
Q: Can I cancel my booking?
A: Yes. We charge nothing for cancelling, but the hotel and airline cancellation policies in force will apply. Message us and we will explain the exact terms for your booking.
Q: Where is your office?
A: Al Raza Travel, Plot No. C3, First Floor, Lane-10, Near Faizan-e-Islam Masjid, Khayaban-e-Sehar, Phase-7, Defence, Karachi. You can also book by phone or WhatsApp.
